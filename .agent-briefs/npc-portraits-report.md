## 1. Bartek — Senior Consultant & Team Lead

- **Name + Role:** Bartek — Senior Consultant (Team Lead)
- **Pose:** 3/4 view facing slightly right, head tilted slightly back with chin raised in mild skepticism, shoulders slouched back into his chair.
- **Expression:** Weary half-smirk, heavy dark circles under hooded eyelids, left eyebrow raised 2 pixels higher than right; projects sardonic patience.
- **Hair Color + Style:** Messy dark ash-brown hair with visible gray flecks at the temples; short scruffy full beard with denser salt-and-pepper stippling around the chin.
- **Skin Tone:** Fair-olive complexion with desaturated undertones from chronic monitor exposure; faint reddish hue across the bridge of the nose from resting glasses.
- **Clothing:** Faded washed slate-gray Oxford button-down shirt, unbuttoned two buttons down at the collar, sleeves rolled up to mid-forearm with visible cuff folds.
- **Accessories:** Thick black rectangular-frame acetate glasses with a single 1-pixel white specular highlight on the upper-left lens rim; white ceramic coffee mug rim peeking at lower frame.
- **Background:** Solid deep desaturated slate-navy (`#1c2833`) with a subtle 2-shade diagonal dither gradient in the top-left corner.
- **Distinctive Feature:** Thick black rectangular glasses framing hooded, tired eyes and salt-and-pepper beard scruff.
- **Color Palette (Hex):** `#1e272e` (Dark Frame/Shade), `#636e72` (Slate Shirt), `#dcdde1` (Highlight/Collar), `#d29971` (Skin Midtone), `#2d3436` (Hair/Beard Base).

### Pixel Artist Guidance (96×96)
Render Bartek with heavy 1-pixel dark contours around the jaw and glasses frame. Use tight 2×2 checkerboard dithering under the chin and along the shirt collar to create texture on his washed-out shirt. His eyes should be narrow (3 pixels high total: 1 dark eyelid line, 1 brown iris pixel, 1 dark lower shadow line) to convey his chronic lack of surprise at office disasters. The salt-and-pepper beard should use scattered single-pixel light-gray dots over a dark brown base rather than solid gray blocks.

### 3. Readability & Scaling Considerations
- **Resolution Budget:** While native portraits are 96×96 pixels, the UI may downsample to 48×48 or 32×32 in quick roster cards. Maintain bold, uncluttered outer silhouette lines. Avoid single-pixel detached noise.
- **Lighting Direction:** Standardize a unified primary light source from the **top-left (approx. 45 degrees)** across all 13 portraits. This ensures that when dialogue boxes pop up, the cast shadows and highlights feel cohesive across the entire cast.
