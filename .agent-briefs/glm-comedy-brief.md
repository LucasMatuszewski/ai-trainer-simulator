Comedy DNA: IT Crowd (Roy, Moss, Jen, Richmond) + Silicon Valley (Richard, Erlich, Dinesh, Gilfoyle). Dry, deadpan, brutally accurate to real IT-folk suffering, occasional absurd escalation.
